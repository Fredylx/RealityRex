# RealityRex Privacy Policy
Last updated: February 11, 2026

## Summary
RealityRex does not collect, store, or share personal data. All AR experiences run locally on your device.

## Camera
The camera is used to provide the augmented reality experience. Camera data is processed on-device and is not stored or transmitted.

## Analytics and Tracking
RealityRex does not use third‑party analytics or tracking SDKs.

## Contact
If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@codbodydynamics.com.
