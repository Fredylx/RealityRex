# RealityRex Support & Terms

  Welcome to RealityRex support. If you need help, you’ll find quick tips and contact info below.

  ## Support
  - Email: support@codebodydynamics.com
  - Please include your device model and iOS version, and describe what you were doing when the issue occurred.

  ## Quick Start
  1. Launch the app and grant camera access.
  2. Move your device to help detect surfaces.
  3. Tap to place the T‑Rex.
  4. Double‑tap a dino to delete it.

  ## FAQs
  - I don’t see any surfaces.
    - Move your device slowly so AR can detect flat surfaces. Try better lighting and textured surfaces (e.g., floors,
  tables).
  - The dino looks too small/large or clips into objects.
    - Try placing on a different surface or adjust distance by moving a step back before tapping.
  - The app asks for camera access.
    - The camera is required for AR. We don’t store or transmit camera data.

  ## Troubleshooting
  - Force‑quit and relaunch the app if tracking seems stuck.
  - Ensure your device supports ARKit and you’re on a supported iOS version.
  - Restart the device if AR tracking doesn’t recover after a few minutes.
